This website

lilylambpress.github.io is a static website with free printable coloring pages. We do not use accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not collect personal information from visitors. Our Pinterest app

Lily & Lamb Press uses a private Pinterest app only to manage its own Pinterest account: creating and scheduling its own Pins and boards and reading its own analytics and keyword trends. The app does not access, collect or store data from other Pinterest users, and it does not sell or share any data. Access tokens are kept in an encrypted credential store on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings.
